# Lab 03 — Password Reset and Sign-in Management

## Objective

The objective of this lab is to practise common Microsoft 365 account support tasks related to password resets and sign-in access.

This lab focuses on resetting a user password, blocking a user from signing in, unblocking the user, and documenting these actions as part of an IT Support workflow.

## What I Learned

In this lab, I learned how to manage basic account access issues in the Microsoft 365 Admin Centre.

I practised resetting a user password, which is a common first-line IT Support task when users cannot access their account.

I also practised blocking and unblocking sign-in. Blocking sign-in can be useful when an account may be compromised, when a user leaves the organisation, or when temporary access removal is required.

## IT Support Relevance

Password resets and sign-in management are common service desk tasks.

In a workplace environment, users may contact IT Support because they cannot sign in, have forgotten their password, or need their account access reviewed. Support teams may also need to block accounts for security reasons.

Documenting these actions clearly helps create a support history and shows what action was taken on the account.
